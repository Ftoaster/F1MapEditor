Mapeditor – How to use

Quick start

  1. Create a track: Project → New, then Points (P) and click into the map – or From template … / Generate suggestion ….
  2. Close lap (from four points): the toolbar button lights up while the lap is open. The preview is then calculated automatically.
  3. Fine-tuning: drag points, change values on the right under Properties, check heights in the height profile.
  4. Check (Ctrl+Enter): messages below under Check, a click jumps to the location.
  5. Build: creates the package for the Track Installer; then test it in game and note the result under Playtest.

Show and hide

Every function can be shown or hidden: View → map layers (grid, edges, direction, play area, zones, racing line, pit/grid slots/sectors, corner numbers, input points, labels, scale, hint labels), areas (side panel, properties, lower area, banking field, toolbar, header and status bar), individual tabs and automation (racing line, autosave, open last project, start screen). Everything at a glance: Customise view … (Ctrl+Shift+A); Show everything restores everything. The settings are remembered. Ctrl+M or double-clicking a tab maximises the lower area. View → Sprache / Language switches between German and English immediately, without a restart (project, undo history, preview and map view stay).

Left column

Display and the point list open and close with a click on their heading. Long lists start closed and open in the Points tool. Zoom in and Zoom out (also Ctrl + and Ctrl −) are in the View menu. The banking box on the right appears with a selection and in the Banking tab.

Find and recover

Go to (field above the list): metres from the finish (e.g. 1250), corner (T4) or object (#12). The list shows station and corner for each point. Project → Recently opened and the start screen offer the last projects. The editor saves unsaved changes every minute to .mapeditor_wiederherstellung.json in the project folder; after a crash it offers them when opening (restoring can be undone).

Segment tracks

Dragging the end of a segment changes its length or angle, the yellow square on an arc its radius. Left: insert straight/arc, split, change type. For free group editing use “Convert to control points”.

Height and gradient (Height profile tab)

Every control point or segment end is a handle on the height line. Drag horizontally to select the range A→B, drag vertically to change the height (0.1 m grid, one undo step; selected points move together). Buttons above the profile: Smooth transition A→B (S-curve without a kink at the ends), ramp, same height, smooth. ↑/↓ 0.1 m, with Shift 1 m. Right-click: even gradient (ramp between the first and last selected point), same height, smooth, enter height. Between the points the gradient is shown in % (red above 8 %). The mouse wheel zooms horizontally, the middle mouse button pans, a double-click shows the whole lap. The selection is linked with the map and the list.

Banking (tilt to the side)

Select corner(s) or points, set the amount on the right in the Banking field (quick buttons Flat, 2°, 4°, FIA max) and choose the side: Corner inside lower adapts to every corner, for straights left higher or right higher. The change only affects the selection; the generator smooths the transitions. All corners automatically banks the whole track by corner radius (tight = the set amount, flat from R 500 m), All flat removes all banking. Check: the Banking and Cross-section tabs below. In the Banking tab the banking can be edited like the height profile: drag points (for segments the segment middle) vertically, drag horizontally to select a range A→B, ↑↓ changes by 0.1° (with Shift 1°), buttons or right-click: Smooth transition A→B, Ramp, Same value, Smooth, Enter banking …. Every change is one undo step.

Editing the cross-section

Cross-section tab, panel Edit cross-section on the right: range from/to in metres from the finish (buttons Selection A→B or Cursor ±50 m), side (left, right, both in driving direction), then Road (half) for wider or narrower asphalt, Kerb (0 = none), Grass strip, Run-off (grass, gravel or asphalt) and the Barrier distance. Fields left at automatic or unchanged keep the generator's automatic layout (kerbs in corners, gravel after tight corners). Apply sets the section, Automatic removes your own sections in the range. The barrier moves out at most as far as there is room to the next part of the track; at the pit lane, the pit lane takes priority. Asphalt run-off is built in the game with the pit-lane asphalt and the road's driving physics.

Racing line (racing line, braking points, corners)

After every change the generator calculates the racing line in the background (10–50 s depending on the track, switch Calculate racing line automatically on the left; otherwise with Check, Ctrl+Enter). Map: racing line red = braking, yellow = coasting, green = full throttle; red triangles = braking point (with km/h when zoomed in), white squares = distance boards, T1 … = corner numbers as in the game (the build merges more than 15 corners), S2/S3 = sector boundaries, white dots = grid slots, orange = pit lane and pit boxes. Tab Speed: km/h over the lap; Analysis: lap time and corner table (click a row to jump to the corner). After a change the racing line is pale until the new one is ready. The build uses the same result as its pre-check (the same state is not calculated twice).

Pit lane

Choose Properties → Pit lane: squares appear on the map at the entry and exit; dragging moves them along the track (one undo step). The racing line then shows the exact pit lane.

Environment (trees, buildings, tents …)

Tab Environment: catalogue of the game models (search, category, preview, size). Choose a model and click the map to place it (tool U); clicking an object selects it, dragging moves it, a frame selects several, Del deletes, double-click selects the whole group. Tool B plants an area: drag a rectangle or paint with the brush, with density (objects per hectare), model mix (choose several models), random rotation, scale range, minimum distance to the track (default 15 m behind the wall), spacing between objects and a seed (same value = same scatter). Every scatter is a group and one undo step. The objects appear as circles at catalogue size (colour per category; View → Environment hides them). Ground cover keeps the grass and weeds of the carrier. The check reports unknown models and objects outside the track area or on the road (click jumps to the object). The build takes the project's file umgebung.json.

Terrain: In the Terrain box, Place hill puts a hill into the track area with the next click (height −60 … 200 m, negative = hollow; visible and drivable, the build flattens it towards the track). Place mountain puts a mountain outside the track area (zoom out first; 10 … 2000 m high, up to 12 km from the centre; scenery on the horizon only). Dragging the centre moves it, height and radius are changed in the box, Del deletes. Shapes with a red outline are misplaced or are cut off at the edge of the track area.

Build and transfer to the game

Build suggests the next build name (e.g. testedit11) and shows the required and free storage. Track code: every code the game database knows (recommended: sochi); below it you see whether the code is already installed in the game and whether the database is activated. Several custom tracks side by side need different codes. Transfer to game … writes the last built package to F1 25 via quick swap: first the plan, then your confirmation (every time), with a backup of the affected game files under injektion_sicherung next to the working folder beforehand; afterwards the playtest entry opens. The EGO editor does not need to run: if the mod is deactivated there, the transfer restores the remembered database activation itself. Only the first installation of a track code and its first activation (“Activate mod”) go once through the Track Installer or EGO editor. Clean up … (also in the Tools menu) deletes old builds and packages after confirmation.

Range from the selection

If you mark connected points or segments, for example with a frame on the map, the Cross-section tab takes them as its From / to range, also across the finish line. With gaps in the selection the range stays as it is. Input you have not applied yet is kept.

Edge angle

In the Cross-section tab, Edge angle sets what happens beside the road: Level is the default, Same as track continues the banking at every point over the first 12 m, Custom angle uses a fixed value (positive rises outwards). Choose range and side as for the other fields, Whole lap takes the entire track. Then press Apply.

Ground and surroundings

Under Advanced build options, Own ground and surroundings maps replaces the template's aerial photos: the ground comes from the track surfaces, the racing line sits on the asphalt and the surroundings match hills and mountains. Landscape selects grass or desert (no ground cover), Colour tone tints grass and surroundings dry, lush or autumnal. The option is on by default.

Properties

Double-click a value, change it, Enter. Italic = default value (not saved). Clearing the field restores the default. Yes/no and choice fields open a drop-down list. Both comma and point are accepted as decimal separator. For a point or segment the total width is shown (italic: the width there according to the preview, taken from neighbouring points, profile sections or the road); Asymmetric width: yes creates left/right half widths (starting from the current width), no merges them back into the total width. Banking is set in the banking panel above (if you hide it, it returns as a table row). The check list is sorted by error, warning, note, and by station within each level.

Undo and save

Every completed change is one step: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y. Save Ctrl+S, New Ctrl+N, Open Ctrl+O.

Map colours

■ Input (your points, immediately) · ■ computed road with edge strips (pale = outdated, being recalculated) · ■ play area ±1024 m (the track must lie inside it) · ■ Straight Mode · ■ DRS
